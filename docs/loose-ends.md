# Loose ends

Written on 2026-11-13, in the stretch after in-light-of.md.

## The rule

What should be done next is written here, and only here. It is not written
into the other notes.

This file is different from the others. It is meant to be rewritten. When
a loose end is tied, its line is deleted. When one is found, a line is
added. Git keeps what was removed.

## Why

What to do next changes faster than what is understood. Kimbro kept the
two apart: his maps were in ink, and his next steps were on small stickers
that peel off. Here they were mixed. commit-or-it-never-happened.md ended
with three loose ends, and when they were fixed the note had to be
rewritten. the-wiki.md has a "Loose ends" section too.

By "start in the middle" in three-names.md, those two notes are left as
they are. This holds from now on.
