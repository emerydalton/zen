# Loose ends

Written on 2026-11-13, in the stretch after in-light-of.md.

## The rule

What should be done next is written here, and only here. It is not written
into the other notes.

This file is different from the others. It is meant to be rewritten. When
a loose end is tied, its line is deleted. When one is found, a line is
added. Git keeps what was removed.

## Why

What to do next changes faster than what is understood. Kimbro kept the
two apart: his maps were in ink, and his next steps were on small stickers
that peel off. Here they were mixed. commit-or-it-never-happened.md ended
with three loose ends, and when they were fixed the note had to be
rewritten. the-wiki.md has a "Loose ends" section too.

By "start in the middle" in three-names.md, those two notes are left as
they are. This holds from now on.

## The loose ends

- The wiki still holds three pages, Design Goals, Roadmap and FAQ, from
  when /zen was planned as a programming language. Its sidebar lists only
  those pages. (From the-wiki.md.)
- CHANGELOG.md does not mention open-questions.md, the-wiki.md,
  in-light-of.md, three-names.md, what-goes-where.md, questionplace.md,
  ford.md, it-has-to-serve.md, zazen.md or this file.
- docs/README.md says to read every note. It does not say that this file
  is the one that gets rewritten.
- what-goes-where.md lists "what should be done next" as out of docs/ and
  sends it to this file, which is in docs/. It means out of the other
  notes. (Found by a /zendoc.)
- three-names.md says every note in docs/ has one title and one rule. It
  holds three rules itself, and this file and the-wiki.md do not fit
  either. (Found by a /zendoc.)
- in-light-of.md says next steps are written into the notes. That stopped
  being true when this file was added. A fresh reader meets the old claim
  first. (Found by a /zendoc.)
- A run of /zazen is a sesshin; the author described it and the reader
  supplied that word. It has no note. zazen.md could not tell whether a
  sesshin is itself a /zazen.
- The README's "Not two" section and not-two.md still say that one name,
  /zen, covers the span from o7 to o7. zazen.md gives that span its own
  name.
- The README says "three words". Nobody has said whether /zazen is a
  fourth. (From zazen.md.)
- The author began working out a mark called a flag. It says only that a
  thing is here, like a flagpole. A reader may attend to it or not. Some
  of the question marks in what-goes-where.md may be flags instead. The
  author said not to go back and re-mark everything, and not to fold the
  marks into one. It has no note.
- ford.md notices a kind of not understanding that goes away when the
  terms are said more exactly. questionplace.md names two kinds and not
  this one.
- The author said that Eugene Gendlin is one of the largest influences on
  /zen, and that /zen could not be done without him. No note in docs/
  says so. The author left this open as not pressing.
- Four outside documents have been read alongside this repository and
  have no place to live. See what-goes-where.md.

## How this was learned

The reader noticed, while reading Kimbro's summary, that one note here had
already been rewritten because its loose ends were tied. The author called
that an interesting find, and then said to bring the idea in directly.
