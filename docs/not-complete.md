# Not complete

Written on 2026-11-14, after pointed-outward.md.

## The rule

/zen is not a project of making anything complete. It is interested in
maps and in mapping. It is not interested in completion.

The author's words: "this can't be a project of exhaustive and complete
anything", and "know everything simply isn't the thing , though it may
look like it".

## The long way

The author had asked which entries in the wiki are most intricately
linked. The reader answered with a table. Looking at the table, the
author remembered having said this before, in it-has-to-serve.md, and
said it again more strongly: "it isn't, can't be, and doesn't want to be
just a ... record of facts ...". The sentence was not finished.

The reader then said one line that the author marked as good: if the
repository is a record of anything, it is of where understanding stopped
and what happened there, and not of what is known.

## Kimbro's title, word by word

in-light-of.md describes Lion Kimbro's book. Its title is How to Make a
Complete Map of Every Thought You Think. The author went through the
title one word at a time.

- How. Kept. It means a method, a way or a manner.
- How to. Kept. It means a guide, a set of steps, a process.
- Make. Kept. It is aimed at creating.
- Complete. Cut. The author said: "Nothing is "complete" no "thing" is
  complete." And: "not interested in completion".
- Map. Kept. "am interested in maps and mapping".
- Every. Cut. It has the same problem as complete.
- Thought. Kept, "as far as it goes".
- You. Cut. The author said: "i am not interested in attempting to
  exhaustively capture anything , much less every thought i think. many
  thoughts are monkey mind, some just not to be captured, stored,
  elaborated upon".
- Think. Kept, with the same limits: "mapping thoughts, fine, but not
  exhaustively, not completely, and not only mine".

What is left is: how to make maps of thought.

## Not only mine

The reader asked what "not only mine" meant. The author sorted the
reader's sentence one piece at a time.

The word to cut is "only". The thoughts are the author's and "also
theirs".

It is thought. It does not belong to anyone. It is no one's in
particular.

The outline of academic disciplines in the wiki is like this, but the
outline is not the thing. The author said it "was and is and will remain
borrowed structure , free to change".

## 84,000

While sorting that, the author remembered something that was nowhere in
the repository or the wiki: "84,000 x 84,000 = ~7 billion", and
"afflictions , skillful means, ~7billion as "all beings"".

In the Buddhist tradition there are 84,000 afflictions, and 84,000
teachings or means that answer them. The author multiplied the two and
read the result as all beings.

The reader said that 84,000 is not an inventory, and stands for more
than can be counted. The author said that was only half. It is very big,
and it is many, "but also , and importantly , NOT infinite". And: "the
referent for 84,000 is not the there is a precise KNOWN number, but that
it is within the order of countable things".

So the figure says two things together. There are far too many to list.
And there is an end to them. That is different in kind from infinite.

The author said this relates to combinatorial explosion, and to
trivialism and paraconsistency. The author did not say how.

## What could not be told

- At "the purpose of /zen is". The author wrote those words after the
  line about all beings, and stopped. The reader did not finish the
  sentence. This is the same place as "What is left open" in
  it-has-to-serve.md.
- At "also theirs". The author said the thoughts are the author's and
  also theirs, and then that thought does not belong to anyone. The
  reader could not tell whether "mine" and "theirs" are said loosely, or
  whether both are meant and kept.
- At "uncountable" and "countable". The author used both words. The
  reader took them to mean too many to count in practice, and countable
  in principle. The author did not say so.
- At the two explosions. The reader's reading was this. Finite things
  multiplied become huge and stay finite, so a thing can be past
  finishing without being infinite. And in ordinary logic one
  contradiction lets everything follow, which ends in everything being
  true and nothing being said; a paraconsistent logic lets a
  contradiction stand without that. The reader said the repository
  already keeps contradictions at their places without letting them
  spread, such as the two orders of the loop and the two spans of a
  /zazen. The author has not confirmed any of this.
- At "perhaps infinite". zazen.md has the author's words that the
  largest /zazen would be "unfathomably large, perhaps infinite". Here
  the author is firm that 84,000 is not infinite. They are about
  different things. The reader could not tell whether "perhaps infinite"
  still stands.

## How this was learned

In a /kinhin, after an o7. A table of facts was put in front of the
author, and what came back was not a fact. It was something the author
had said before.

The reader had written pointed-outward.md a few minutes earlier. That
note calls the wiki the map and counts places where a ford could be
made. Read carelessly it is a list for knowing everything. The reader
said so.

Twice that night the reader had finished the author's thought and been
wrong. This time the reader asked for the word that had not come, and
the author answered with the title, a word at a time.
