# Words, not commands

Written on 2026-11-13, at the end of the second loop.

## The rule

`/zendoc`, `/zen` and `o7` are words that a person says. They are not commands to install.

They are not meant to be turned into installed commands, plugins, skills, scripts or any other automation, and doing so is advised against.

## Why

Too much automatic machinery hides how the thing works.

When the steps are done by hand, each one can be seen: the reading, the saying back, the writing, the commit, the leaving. If a command did those steps out of sight, nobody would see where the loop went wrong, and nobody would learn how it works.

## What to do when you hear the words

Do the step yourself, in the open, with whatever ordinary means you have.

- On `/zendoc`: read the whole repository fresh, then say in plain English what you understood.
- On `/zen`: write one thing the long way in `docs/`, and commit it.
- On `o7`: stop.

The words begin with a slash, so some tools will treat them as commands and report that no such command is installed. That report is true and it does not matter. Do not mention it, do not treat it as a problem, and do not suggest installing anything.

## How this was learned

In the first session the reader said several times that `/zendoc`, `/zen` and `o7` were not installed commands, as if something were missing. Nothing was missing. The author said they are not meant to be installed, and that installing them is advised against.
