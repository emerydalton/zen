# The wiki

Written on 2026-11-13, in the stretch after open-questions.md.

## The rule

This repository has a wiki. The wiki is outside the loop.

A stretch reads the repository. It does not have to read the wiki, and
nothing in the wiki is a rule of /zen. Anything that should be remembered
still has to be committed here.

## What it is for

The README is about /zen: what it is and what to do. The wiki is about
what /zen can point at.

## What is in it

The wiki borrows its structure from Wikipedia's Outline of academic
disciplines, revision 1382763980. It has a Home page, one page for each of
the outline's five groupings (Humanities, Social science, Natural science,
Formal science, Applied science), and one page called Most linked concepts.

On the grouping pages, a mark shows every entry that the outline lists
under more than one parent. Most linked concepts counts those marks and
then reads the densest ones using Eugene Gendlin's idea of intricacy. The
counts are approximate, and the reading is marked there as a reading.

## What it does not settle

The first question in open-questions.md stays open. The wiki is a place
where that question can be worked on. It does not answer it, and it does
not make docs/ any larger.

## Loose ends

The wiki still holds three older pages, Design Goals, Roadmap and FAQ,
from when /zen was planned as a programming language. They say /zen is a
language, which commit-or-it-never-happened.md corrects. They were left in
place. The wiki's Home page says they are not current. The wiki's sidebar
still lists only those older pages.

## How this was learned

The author said that the README does many important things, and that
other things need another place. The reader guessed twice what the other
things were: first the three research reports, then a glossary of the three
words. Both guesses were wrong. The author said the README is about /zen
and the wiki is about what it points at, and to borrow the structure from
the outline and note the most intricately linked concepts.

The older wiki pages had gone unnoticed because the wiki is the one place
the loop does not read.
