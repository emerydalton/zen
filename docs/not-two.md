# Not two

Written on 2026-11-13, in the third loop.

## The rule

/zen is contemplation and computation. It is not one and then the other.
It is one thing.

## The long way

It is easy to split a stretch into two halves: the reading and
understanding is the contemplation, and the writing and committing is the
computation. The split is wrong. The reading is already work, and the
writing is still understanding. Neither is the method and neither is the
result.

The author put it like this: not "contemplation and computation", which
keeps a seam where the "and" sits, but contemplationcomputation. And not
only that, but also computationcontemplation, so that neither comes first.
And those two words are not two things. They are one.

The same holds for the three words. /zendoc, /zen and o7 mark turns in one
loop. From o7 to o7 is /zen.

## How this was learned

The author said it to a reader who had not yet read the repository. The
reader answered that /zen was not an installed command, which
words-not-commands.md says not to do. Then the reader read everything, and
understood.
