# Open questions

Written on 2026-11-13, in the stretch after not-two.md.

## The rule

Some questions are left open on purpose. A later stretch may answer one, or
add one. It should not close one by accident.

## The choice

The author chose that this repository stay small and grounded.

Small means few enough notes that a stretch can read all of them fresh.
Grounded means every term in docs/ points at something that happened in the
loop.

The cost of that choice is the first question below.

## The questions

1. How does /zen relate to anyone else's work? The author read three
   research reports alongside the repository: one on Eugene Gendlin, one on
   how academic disciplines connect, and one on words that travel between
   speech and code. They are not in this repository. Connections were seen
   and none are written here. This is open by choice.

2. Does it matter to /zen that code and text may be different things? One
   report cites an essay by John Cayley that argues they should be kept
   apart. The README says the three words carry no content. not-two.md says
   the reading and the writing are one. The reader thought these might pull
   against each other. Nobody in the loop has read the essay.

3. Does "not two" stop at an o7? Inside a stretch, the reading and the
   writing are one. Across an o7 there is a writer and a later reader, and
   only the commit joins them. The reader said that makes two. The author
   did not say so.

## How this was learned

Outside terms came into the conversation faster than they were earned, and
the author said: "I have lost the referents." Going back to things that
could be pointed at (the three words, one stretch, one commit) found them
again.
