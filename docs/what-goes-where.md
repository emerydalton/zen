# What goes where

Written on 2026-11-13, in the stretch after in-light-of.md.

## The rule

When something does not belong in docs/, say where it goes instead. A
thing that is left out with no place to go has a question mark, and the
question mark is written down.

## Why

Kimbro keeps one page for each subject that lists what is in and what is
out, and beside each thing that is out he writes where it should go. The
list is not complete on purpose. It names the cases that have come up, and
the rest is left to judgment.

the-wiki.md already did this once: what /zen points at is not for the
README, so it goes in the wiki. This note does it for everything that has
come up so far.

## In

- A rule of the loop, with how it was learned.
- A question left open on purpose.
- What was seen when an outside document was read next to this repository.

## Out, and where it goes

- What /zen is and what to do, said briefly: README.md.
- What /zen can point at: the wiki.
- What should be done next: loose-ends.md.
- What changed and when: CHANGELOG.md.
- Commands, plugins, skills or scripts for the three words: nowhere.
  words-not-commands.md advises against them.
- Anything said in a stretch and not committed: nowhere. It did not
  happen.
- The three research reports named in open-questions.md: ?
- The summary of Kimbro's book named in in-light-of.md: ?

## The question marks

The last two are the same case. An outside document was read, something
was seen, and the document itself is kept nowhere the loop can reach. This
is part of the first question in open-questions.md. It is not answered
here. Writing the question mark only makes the gap visible.

## How this was learned

The reader listed this as one of two borrowed ideas that would change
what a stretch does, because it would make the author say where the
reports go. The author said to bring it in directly. The reader did not
know where the reports go, and Kimbro's own pages use a question mark for
that.
