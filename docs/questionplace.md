# Questionplace

Written on 2026-11-13, in the stretch after what-goes-where.md.

## The rule

A questionplace is a notunderstanding, kept.

The author's word is questionplace, and also placequestion, so that neither
half comes first. It is a question that can only be asked from one spot,
and a spot that can only be seen because something could not be answered
there. They are not two things.

Kept means committed. Nothing else counts as kept here.

## Two kinds of not understanding

The README says that if a /zendoc comes back wrong, the docs were unclear,
and the next /zen fixes them. That is one kind. The docs were at fault, and
the fix removes it.

The other kind is not a fault. The reader read carefully and still could
not tell. That one is not fixed. It is kept, with where it happened.

So a reader who does not understand should say which kind it is: "the docs
were unclear here", or "I could not tell, and here is where".

A notunderstanding that is fixed was a defect. One that is neither fixed
nor kept is lost, and by commit-or-it-never-happened.md it never happened.
Only the kept one is a questionplace.

## What to do

When you cannot tell, say where. Write the spot down. Do not resolve it to
make the note tidier.

There is no separate loop for finding these. Ordinary reading finds them.

## How this file is kept

This file grows. A questionplace is not deleted when someone answers it.
The answer is written beside it and the place stays. That is the opposite
of loose-ends.md, where a line is deleted when it is done.

If one of these grows until it needs a rule of its own, it gets its own
note then, and not before.

## The questionplaces

1. Where /zen writes long and /zendoc says it back short. Kimbro's loop has
   two phases, long and then short. not-two.md says there is one thing. The
   reader could not tell whether these disagree. (First written in
   in-light-of.md.)

2. Where every note in docs/ is about the loop. Kimbro counts that as a way
   his system fails. The author said that whether it is a failure here is
   unknown, because the author has no felt sense of it yet.

3. Where the wiki borrows a structure and reads it for the places it
   breaks. The reader went to the wiki when the author asked, and tried to
   say what it is in Kimbro's terms. It is not his mirror of one mind,
   because it holds Wikipedia's subjects and not the author's. It is not
   the correct map of everything that he warns against, because its Home
   page says the structure is borrowed and not asserted. He has no category
   for it. The author had a hunch that docs/ does one part of Kimbro's
   system and the wiki does the other. This is where that hunch stopped.

   A ford was later marked at this place. See ford.md. The place stays.

Two older ones were here before the word. The third question in
open-questions.md is asked at an o7 and nowhere else. The question marks
in what-goes-where.md sit beside the outside documents. They stay where
they are.

## How this was learned

Three times in one evening the reader or the author could not tell
something, and each time the author said that the not being able to tell
was itself information. The third time the reader listed the three and
said they were less like questions than like places. The author answered
with the word, both ways round. Then: "questionplace is notunderstanding",
and on the next line, "kept". The author counted three notunderstandings
and no questionplaces, asked whether that needed three notes or one, and
said /zen.
