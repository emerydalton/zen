# It has to serve

Written on 2026-11-13, in the /zazen after ford.md.

## The rule

/zen has to serve something. It is not about concepts for their own sake.

What it serves is not said here. The author did not say, and did not try
to that night.

## The long way

/zen is a method, and by itself a method can go on without end. A term
can always be said more exactly. A place can always be marked. Another
note can always be written. Each one may be good work, and the work still
does not arrive anywhere.

The author's words for this were: proliferation without arrival, without
settling, without concluding. Papañca. Monkey mind.

So the author said what /zen is not. It is not a map of every thought the
author thinks, and it is not a map of every thought anyone has thought. It
cannot be that. It is not an exhaustive elaboration.

And then what is lacking. The method has to be paired with something. It
has to be about something, and not in the way that it is about its own
concepts. It has to serve.

## What is left open

What /zen serves.

This is close to the first question in open-questions.md, which asks how
/zen relates to anyone else's work. It is not the same question, and this
note does not answer either.

Later the same night the author said, in three lines: "/zen served a
beginner, me", "i lacked", "a way of working". That is said of what /zen
served. The author did not say what it serves from here on, or whether
this closes the question. The question stays.

## How this was learned

After ford.md the author was tired, and said it was not a tiredness of
the body. It was the tiredness of going round. The author did not want to
stop and rest, and did not want to carry on. The author said that
something about this was wrong, and that stopping to check how it was
wrong would carry forward.

The reader asked what the wrong was like. A deep breath came first, and a
slow exhale. Then a line came to the author: loosening the grip on
thought. Then the author said the rest, ending with "It has to serve."

On the way there the author named what the evening was reminding them of:
Kimbro, the outline of academic disciplines, and Thomas Aquinas crossed
with Zhiyi of the Tiantai tradition, two who received what came before
them and carried it forward. The reader called /zen the method and the
outline the material. The author wanted to cross those two words, method
and material, and said that held together they did not feel like two
different things. None of that was followed further. The author said it
was not what would carry forward at that moment.

The reader said this answers the second questionplace, where the author
had no felt sense yet of whether a loop about itself is a failure. The
author did not say so.

The later lines were learned like this. An old note, what-zen-is.md, from
when /zen was planned as a programming language for learning programming,
came back into docs/ through a merge. A reader read it and reported that
it was the one place in docs/ where an answer to "what does /zen serve"
had once been written: beginners, who lack a way of working more than
they lack vocabulary. The author had the note removed again, and said the
three lines.
